# Brand-first web build

Generated sites share a recognisable look because every choice defaults: indigo gradients, three equal cards, a badge above each heading, Inter, centred everything, fade-ins on scroll. Avoiding the defaults is not enough; something has to take their place. That something is a brief and one visual idea taken from the brand's own world. Do those first, then build.

## Workflow

1. **Collect what exists.** Name, logo, colours, product list, prices, places, real quotes, photos. Ask only for what blocks the work (at most one short question); otherwise proceed and list gaps in the report. Never invent facts: statistics, customer counts, testimonials, logos, awards, prices.
2. **Write the direction.** Fill `references/direction-worksheet.md`. The visual idea matters most: a coffee roaster by a harbour might use a ledger with dotted leaders and a huge wordmark; a clinic might use a printed appointment slip. It must appear in at least two places and never as clip-art.
3. **Choose structure from content, not from a template.**
   - Prices and specs: ledger or table. Reasons or steps: numbered list. Social proof: one large quote plus small ones, or fewer quotes with real attribution.
   - Alternate quiet and loud sections; no two neighbours share a skeleton. Start-align text, centre one deliberate moment.
   - One primary action, named by outcome ("اطلب أول كيس", not "ابدأ الآن").
4. **Build as a static page unless told otherwise** (one HTML file, CSS in a `<style>` block, fonts self-hosted in `fonts/`). If the user publishes through an artifact tool, the same rules apply inside it. Rules, with reasons:
   - Colour from the brand, never stock Tailwind/Bootstrap values. Body 4.5:1, large 3:1, all states.
   - Two families max with roles: expressive display for wordmark and h1/h2, calm text face for the rest. h1 at least 2.5x body, 1.25x steps, weight or colour contrast with body.
   - 4px spacing tokens as CSS variables, then only variables.
   - Motion: none or one purposeful moment; transition named properties; `prefers-reduced-motion`; visible `:focus-visible`.
   - Copy states facts. No "unlock", "seamless", "افتح قوة", "بجودة لا مثيل لها". At most one em dash.
   - Responsive from 375 px. Check mobile before desktop polish.
5. **Arabic or RTL?** Read `references/rtl-arabic.md` before writing CSS: Arabic-designed fonts, no letter-spacing, logical properties, one numeral system, display faces only at large sizes.
6. **Verify.**
   - `python3 scripts/lint_ai_tells.py index.html`: target 0 of 16 original tells. Explain any extra hit you keep.
   - Screenshot at 375, 768, 1280 px and look at them: overflow, stranded items, unreadable display type, empty columns. Fix and re-check once.
7. **Report** with `references/report-template.md`, trimmed to what applies to a new build: direction, linter result, and a "needs your input" list (photography, real testimonials, logo files, missing facts).

## Judgment calls

- Existing brand guidelines override palette and type suggestions here.
- If the product is visual and there are no images, say so. Do not fill the gap with gradients, blobs, or emoji.
- A clean linter result only proves the known tells are gone. Ask of the screenshot: could this page belong to any other brand? If yes, strengthen the visual idea.

## Files

- `scripts/lint_ai_tells.py`: 28-pattern linter (`--json`, `--v1-only`)
- `references/patterns.md`: each tell, why it reads as AI, what to use instead
- `references/direction-worksheet.md`: the brief to fill first
- `references/rtl-arabic.md`: RTL and Arabic checklist
- `references/report-template.md`: report format
